# Release Boundary

Keep public code and private delivery assets separate.

Allowed in this repository:

- Generic Python source, tests, documentation, and CI configuration.
- Freshly authored demonstration Markdown, templates, and images.
- Profile schemas and validation contracts.

Keep out until a rights review approves them:

- Customer or course reports and their source Markdown.
- Institutional, competition, or employer Word templates.
- Screenshots, charts, logos, and source images from project work.
- MathType OLE objects, formula banks, installers, and license files.

Native MathType support must remain opt-in. It must require the operator to
provide their own authorized Word, MathType, template, and formula bank.
